FaceOff Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceOff ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS app and FaceOff sensor device. We respect your privacy and have built FaceOff to keep your data on your device whenever possible.

Information we collect

The FaceOff app collects only the information needed to provide the service:

  • Sensor data: When you pair a FaceOff sensor and start a training session, the app receives motion data (accelerometer and gyroscope readings) from the sensor over Bluetooth. This data is used to detect clamps, count reps, and save your session history.
  • Pairing information: The app stores a unique identifier for each FaceOff sensor you pair, so it can reconnect automatically next time you open the app.
  • Profile information: If you enter a name or set training goals in the app, this is stored locally on your device.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: If the app crashes, we may collect anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs. These reports do not include personal information.

Where your data is stored

All of your sensor data, session history, and profile information is stored locally on your iPhone. If you enable iCloud sync in your iOS settings, your data may also be backed up to your personal iCloud account, which only you can access. We do not run our own servers to store your data, and we never receive a copy of it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or have access to your:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or location
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera
  • Login credentials or payment information
  • Health or fitness data outside of the FaceOff sensor
  • Social media or third-party accounts

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Crash reports, if collected, are sent only to our crash reporting service (Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google) and contain no personal information.

Children's privacy

FaceOff is designed for use by athletes of various ages, including minors. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to remove that information.

Bluetooth permission

FaceOff requires Bluetooth permission to communicate with your FaceOff sensor. This permission is used solely to connect to and receive data from the sensor. We do not use Bluetooth to track your location or connect to other devices.

Your choices

  • You can delete all your local data at any time by deleting the FaceOff app from your iPhone.
  • You can revoke Bluetooth permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
